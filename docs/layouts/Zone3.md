# Zone 3: Middle Banks

Drawn as one 720 x 608 piece by `tools/gen/shore/gen.py` (seeded; edit the generator and rerun). No rock shapes, nothing over 7 studs (the banks are 5.8), nothing cut deeper than 2.

## The frame

- **Four long banks** (a crest 5.8 high and 22 wide with worn edges, between two shallow runnels) wind across the beach at about z 200, 320, 440 and 555. Each has an open gap and a low saddle (3.2 high) that people walk over but carts can't. The gaps zigzag: west (x -135 to -85) in rows 1 and 3, east (x 85 to 135) in rows 2 and 4. Saddles: x 190 to 240 in rows 1 and 3, x -250 to -200 in rows 2 and 4. Three more banks sit near the top. Together they hide 85 to 90% of the lower half from the top edge.
- **The creek:** a shallow channel (1.6 deep, 16 wide) that finds the gaps, running from the top down through each in turn and ending in a wide shallow mouth (a 26-radius hollow with weed) near (140, 590).
- **The old wreck:** half-buried ribs, timbers and crates in a shallow hollow at (235, 380), between rows 2 and 3.
- **Wrack lines** of weed, shells, driftwood and pebbles at about z 270, 390 and 510, in broken runs; shell, pebble, weed and starfish scatters; two outcrops of decor rocks; dune grass and pebbles on the banks.
- The rock pool at (-60, 130) and the pier's line (x 40, z 0 to 90) stay clear.

## Crossings

1. **Down the gaps:** a cart ramp through each row's gap in turn, west, east, west, east, following the creek.
2. **Across the banks:** every gap is plugged with a bar, and the ramp goes through the banks on the opposite side (east, west, east, west), cutting them.

## What floods first

The creek mouth and the creek's lower reaches, then the runnels beside the banks, seaward ones before landward.

## Times

Map tool 30 s, Test Range 31 s for both crossings, against a target of 45 s. I couldn't lengthen it: nothing a zone 3 layout may build (7 studs tall, 4 deep, no walls) stops a walker on the real terrain, whose 4-stud cells round the banks off (taller and narrower crests also came out 3 studs lower than drawn), so the straight way down is always open on foot. The banks stop carts, which must keep to the gaps and the ramp, and they hide the way ahead as the zone should. Shortcut starts 5 of 5.
