# Zone 1: Shopside Shore

A sunny, open, readable shore, drawn as one 720 x 608 piece by `tools/gen/shore/gen.py` (seeded, so the layout is stable; edit the generator and rerun it). No rock, nothing taller than 2.5 studs, nothing cut deeper than 1.4.

## The frame

- **Dunes:** twelve low curving dunes (2 to 2.5 high, 16 wide, worn edges), most of them in the top third, with open sand between. Their sides are about 0.46 steep: steeper than a cart climbs (0.35), easy on foot. So carts take the ramps and players cut across the dunes.
- **The creek:** a shallow channel (1.4 deep, 14 wide) winding from near the top to the sea, with damp grey sand along it.
- **Tide pools:** three round hollows with salt sand, pebbles, rocks and starfish round them.
- **Wrack lines:** three wavering lines across the beach (about z 250, 410 and 520) of weed, shells, driftwood and pebbles, broken into runs with gaps.
- **Firm sand:** a wavy band of damp sand along the seaward edge, a pebble bank (slate) mid-zone, dune grass on every dune, shell scatters, a crate, a few rocks.
- **Kept flat:** the boardwalk's walkway (x -16 to 16, z 0 to 24) and the four umbrella camps at (-265, 24), (-120, 32), (170, 26) and (290, 36): no shapes, no decor within 18 studs.

## Crossings

1. **West road:** a cart ramp swaying down the west side (x -170 to -282).
2. **East road:** a cart ramp swaying down the east side (x 150 to 270).

Both ramps wander about 1.2 times the straight way; players take the straight way over the dunes.

## What floods first

The creek and the tide pools hold the sea longest; the creek's lower reaches go under first, then the pools.

## Times

Map tool and Test Range: fastest on foot about 30 s against a target of 35 s (14% under: there's nothing on a shore this gentle that a player can't walk over, so the straight way is the fast way). Shortcut starts 4 of 5 (west road) and 3 of 5 (east road); hidden from the top 2 to 4%.
