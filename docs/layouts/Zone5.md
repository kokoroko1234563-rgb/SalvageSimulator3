# Zone 5: The Shoals

A whole-zone layout (720 x 608): flooded ground between four low rock bars. Every tide the zone rolls one of five crossings and a room for each of seven side slots. Target: 65 s on foot.

## The frame (always there)

- **Four bars** across the beach at z 124, 248, 372 and 496 (12 wide, 14 tall: too high to jump or see over). Each has five doors, 28 wide, at x -280, -144, 0, 144 and 280. A crossing plugs all but two doors on each bar.
- **The Grotto**: a roofed tunnel across the middle lane (z 282 to 338, x -62 to 62), 32 wide inside, open at both ends. The landmark of the zone and its main shelter; the best frame loot lies in it. It floods with the lane.
- Boulders (rock mounds) scattered in the lanes, and a few frame loot spots in the corners of the lanes.
- Five lanes: the top lane (z 0 to 118), then three between the bars, then the bottom lane (z 502 to 608).

## The crossings

Each crossing keeps two doors on every bar: a **ford** for feet (a cut 4 deep with a sand sill across it, a scramble that a cart cannot take) and the door the **cart ramp** uses. The ramp is routed around the Grotto and every slot. Each crossing also lays a reef across the top lane and another across the bottom lane, with a gap for feet (off the ford, so the first and last stretch wind) and a wide gap where the ramp cuts through. Each ford has a wet approach (a shallow cut) that floods before the dry ground.

## The slots (7) and their rooms

Each slot is 100 x 64 against a bar, a walled pocket with a door in its far wall. Rooms: **Drowned pool** (a hollow, floods first), **Dead-end cave** (a roofed gallery along the door, a chamber behind it with the best loot), **Boulder scramble** (rock mounds), **Weed-choked channel** (a cut with sand banks).

1. Weed cove (x -260, z 56, above bar 1): pool, cave, weeds
2. Eastern shelf (x 160, z 128, below bar 1): scramble, pool, cave
3. Reef cave (x -120, z 128, below bar 1): cave, scramble, pool
4. Drowned yard (x 160, z 252, below bar 2): weeds, pool, scramble
5. Western hollow (x -260, z 376, below bar 3): pool, weeds, cave
6. Stack gallery (x 20, z 376, below bar 3): scramble, cave, weeds
7. Outer cove (x -120, z 500, below bar 4): cave, pool, scramble, weeds

## Flood order

The fords are cut 4 deep and their approaches 2, the pools 4, so they fill well before open ground. The seaward fords (bar 4, then bar 3) go under first, then the bar 2 and bar 1 fords, so on the way back up the quick way closes behind you from the sea end. The pools and weed channels in the rooms and the Grotto's roofed floor go with the lanes. The Test Range (crossing 5, first rooms) puts about 46% of the zone under water with the waterline at the zone's middle and 97% at its top, with half the tunnels flooded at the middle.

## Measured times

Target 65 s (55 to 75 within 15%). The map tool (4-stud steps along X and Z only) gives about 64 to 69 s per crossing (shortcut starts 5 of 5). Test Range (walking the real terrain), fastest way down: crossing 1: 59 s, 2: 59 s, 3: 60 s, 4: 58 s, 5: 62 s. The way back up takes the same.

## What a player sees from the top

Nothing of the lower half (hidden share 97 to 100%, from the map tool).

## Notes

- The Test Range reports no problems for this zone with every crossing and every room. (Its only report is the Far Sandbar's `GlyphSlab4` floating 1.2 studs, which is not part of this layout.)
- Walls' ends sit at positions where the Test Range's terrain samples would catch an end's rounding (a gap's left end at 2 mod 8, its right end at 6 mod 8); doors are at multiples of 8 for that reason.
