# Zone 4: Outer Shore / Reef (base)

Rock spines with narrow gaps, boulders in the corridors and a sea cave made of two tall spines. The seaward channel floods first. Rock is only in this zone, and the tallest shapes (10 studs) are here.

## Flood order

As the tide comes in, each cut floods once the waterline falls to its floor. Deeper and further-out cuts flood first.

<!-- floods Zones/Zone4@4 -->
Channel at x 100, z 132 floods when the waterline is below Z 632
Hollow at x -40, z 66 floods when the waterline is below Z 549
Hollow at x 200, z 62 floods when the waterline is below Z 545
<!-- /floods -->

## Shortcuts

Shortcut starts counts how many of five start points on the top edge reach the sea faster on foot than by cart. The ramps are the cart route, and gaps and steep banks are the foot route.

## What a player sees from the top

Mostly hidden from the top. The gaps in the spines are the only way through for players on foot.
