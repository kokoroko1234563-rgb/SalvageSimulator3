# Zone 4: Outer Shore

One 720 x 608 piece (no tiling). Three long rock bars run right across the beach, side pockets are walled off down both edges, and each tide one of five crossings decides how you get through. Rock starts here: mostly open sky, with a handful of short roofed passages as landmarks. Zones 5 and 6 still use their old strips.

Top-down, the top edge at the top, the sea at the bottom. Legend: `#` rock, `^` raised sand, `.` flat, `~` floods at mid-tide, `=` cart ramp, `o` entrance flat, `R` roofed.

## The frame (always there)

- **Three bars** at z 146, 294 and 442 (16 wide, 20 to 22 tall), running wall to wall. They have no doors of their own: every crossing cuts its own with its cart ramp.
- **The Arch**: a roofed passage through the middle bar at x 2 (32 wide between its walls, about 64 long, roof 12 up). Four crossings plug it with a wall, which leaves two dark roofed alcoves either side of the plug (small loot in each); the Arch road drives through it.
- **Side walls** at x -246 and 246 in the three lower lanes, each with a 28-wide door onto a pocket (six pockets, the slots).
- Lane 0 (above the first bar) is open sky; the three lower lanes are the maze.
- Frame loot: the two Arch alcoves, two far corners of the top lane. One entrance flat at x 0, z 100.

## The five crossings

All five cut the three bars at different places and all have a cart ramp from the top to the bottom edge. Each also has a **scramble bank** in the top lane (a low sand bank a player climbs but a cart can't): the ramp loops round its end, so players far from the ramp's start are quicker on foot.

1. **West gallery** (doors at x -210, 100, -150): a 36-wide roofed gallery (60 studs long) in lane 1 you can walk instead of the ramp's open route; a chicane in lane 2 (down one side of a spur, back up the corridor between it and a second spur).
2. **East boulder run** (doors at x 200, -120, 170): a boulder cluster in lane 1; the lane 2 chicane runs the other way, in a 48-wide corridor.
3. **Spine walk** (doors at x -205, -60, 190): two overlapping tall spurs in lane 1 wind the way down, up and out; a short roofed corridor in the last lane.
4. **Arch road** (doors at x 200, 2, -200): the one that goes through the Arch. Pools in lane 1 (they flood first), then a westward chicane after the Arch.
5. **Cave road** (doors at x 0, -200, 60): a lane 1 chicane, a pool, then a roofed cave chamber (60 wide, 48 long) in lane 2 that the ramp drives through.

## The side slots

Six pockets (96 wide, 124 or 140 deep), each reached by the door in a side wall, each with three or four rooms. West ones open east, east ones are mirrored. Most of the zone's loot is in them, the best (weight 4 to 5) at the back.

| Slot | Rooms |
|---|---|
| West lagoon (x -350, z 158) | Sheltered cove, Switchback cove, Flooded pocket |
| East lagoon (x 254, z 158) | Boulder scramble, Sea cave, Sheltered cove |
| West caves (x -350, z 306) | Sea cave, Boulder scramble, Switchback cove |
| East caves (x 254, z 306) | Flooded pocket, Sheltered cove, Switchback cove |
| West reef (x -350, z 458) | Switchback cove, Sea cave, Boulder scramble, Flooded pocket |
| East reef (x 254, z 458) | Sea cave, Sheltered cove, Boulder scramble |

- **Sheltered cove**: two arms of rock and a shallow pool at the back.
- **Boulder scramble**: a ring of boulders to climb or squeeze between, loot in the middle.
- **Sea cave**: a roofed chamber with a pool, closed at the back.
- **Flooded pocket**: a big 3-deep pool with boulders at its mouth.
- **Switchback cove**: two staggered spurs, the way in winds up and down between them.

## Flood order

The pools and pockets (hollows) flood first, the lowest in the lane 3 pockets and the Arch road's pools in lane 1. The Arch and the roofed rooms flood with the ground under them; the cave chamber in crossing 5 and the sea caves go once the sea reaches the lane. The lower the room, the earlier: lane 3 pockets, then lane 2, then lane 1.
