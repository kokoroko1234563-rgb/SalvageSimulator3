# Zone 2: Sunny Stretch

Drawn as one 720 x 608 piece by `tools/gen/shore/gen.py` (seeded; edit the generator and rerun). No rock shapes, nothing over 5 studs, nothing cut deeper than 2.

## The frame

- **Three long sandbars** (a crest 4.2 high and 18 wide between two shallow runnels) wind across the beach at about z 200, 330 and 460, with worn edges. Each has an open gap, and the gaps zigzag: row 1's is the pier's (x 2 to 78: the pier at x = 40, z 0 to 330, stays clear of shapes), row 2's is west (x -175 to -125), row 3's east (x 125 to 175). Each row also has a low saddle (3 high) that people walk over and carts can't: x -260 to -215 in rows 1 and 3, x 215 to 260 in row 2. The bars' sides are steeper than a cart climbs (0.35).
- **Low curving bars and runnels:** ten shorter bars (2.6 high), seven of them between the rows and three near the zone's ends with a shallow runnel (1.5 deep) in damp grey sand beside them.
- **Outcrops:** five clusters of decor rocks with barnacles, weed and starfish.
- **Wrack lines** of weed, shells and driftwood at about z 270, 390 and 560; shell, pebble and weed scatters; dune grass on the bars.
- The rock pools at (-280, 20) and (140, 120) are left alone.

## Crossings

1. **Pier, then west, then east:** the cart ramp keeps to the pier's gap through row 1, swings west to row 2's gap, then east to row 3's.
2. **Pier, then east, then west:** rows 2 and 3's gaps are plugged with bars and the ramp opens them on the other sides (east in row 2, west in row 3).

## What floods first

The runnels beside the bars go under first, the seaward ones before the landward ones.

## Times

Map tool and Test Range about 30 s for both crossings, against a target of 40 s. I couldn't lengthen it: nothing a zone 2 layout may build (5 studs tall, 4 deep, no walls) stops a walker on the real terrain, whose 4-stud cells round the bars off, so the straight way down is always open on foot. The bars still stop carts (carts follow the gaps and the ramp) and hide most of the lower half (87% by the map tool); shortcut starts 5 of 5.
