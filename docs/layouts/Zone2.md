# Zone 2: Sunny Stretch

Drawn as one 720 x 608 piece by `tools/gen/shore/gen.py` (seeded; edit the generator and rerun). No rock shapes, nothing over 5 studs, nothing cut deeper than 1.5.

## The frame

- **Two long sandbars** (5 high, 18 wide, worn edges) wind across the beach at about z 200 and z 410, each with an open gap and a low saddle (3 high) that only people can walk over. Their sides are steeper than a cart climbs, so a cart goes through the gaps or along its ramp. Row 1's gap is the pier's: the pier at x = 40 (z 0 to 330) stays clear of shapes. Row 2's gap is at x -135 to -45.
- **Low curving bars and runnels:** nine shorter bars (2.6 high) with a shallow runnel (1.5 deep) alongside each, in damp grey sand, between and beyond the rows.
- **Outcrops:** four clusters of decor rocks with barnacles, weed and starfish, at (-215, 330), (215, 500), (-95, 150) and (130, 260).
- **Wrack lines** of weed, shells and driftwood at about z 320 and z 525; shell, pebble and weed scatters; dune grass on the bars.
- The rock pools at (-280, 20) and (140, 120) are left alone.

## Crossings

1. **Pier and the west gap:** the cart ramp keeps to the pier's gap through the first row, then swings west to row 2's open gap at x -90.
2. **Pier and the east ford:** row 2's west gap is plugged with a bar, and the ramp goes through row 2 at x 150 instead (cleared through the bar).

## What floods first

The runnels beside the bars go under first, the seaward ones before the landward ones.

## Times

Map tool and Test Range: about 30 s for both crossings against a target of 40 s. That's short, and I couldn't lengthen it: on the 4-stud walking grid nothing 5 studs tall (the zone's limit) or 4 deep can stop a walker without trapping them, so the straight way is always open on foot (the bars are a cart obstacle and a scramble, not a wall). Shortcut starts 5 of 5; hidden from the top 68 to 75%.
