# Fog Bank (biome, uncommon; zones 2 to 6)

**Identity.** A wall of fog has rolled in and stays: you see a few studs, then nothing. Now and
then a foghorn sounds from a lighthouse and the fog lifts for a moment: learn the way in that
moment.

**Look.** Grey-white haze, colour washed out. Decor: marker posts, a lighthouse (the landmark),
rope lines between posts, bells.

**The frame.** Open dunes in zones 2 and 3; in zones 4 to 6 low walls and dead ends you can't see
until you're in them. Rope lines along the safe ways.
- **Madame Gull** (the fortune-telling seagull, trader) sets up here.

**The paths.** Two or three per crossing; the fog makes them hard to find, not long: follow the
ropes (safe, slow), or cut across between foghorns.

**Dangers.** **The fog** (a new hazard kind): it thickens to a few studs' sight, and a
**foghorn pulse** clears it for a few seconds every so often. In zones 4 to 6 it hides the
other dangers: covered pits, and the tide coming in.

**Rooms and loot.** Rewards (its exotic secret): Lantern of Clear Sight, Foghorn.
**Keep.** The Lighthouse Key unlocks its secret.

**Questions.**
1. Should a lantern push the fog back a little round you?
