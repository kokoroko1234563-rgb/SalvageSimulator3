# Spearstone Reef (biome, rare; zones 4 to 6)

**Identity.** A black reef of basalt with spears of stone that shoot up from the floor on a
rhythm: a test of timing, the reef's halls ticking like a clock.

**Look.** Rock: Basalt; floor: dark sand and stone. Cold blue haze, contrast up. Decor: broken
spearstones, barnacles, bones.

**The frame.** Reef halls and corridors; spear fields across them.

**The paths.** Two or three per crossing: through the spear fields (quick, timed), or round
them (long).

**Dangers.** **Spears** (a deadly version of the existing Pulse): a field of spots that
glow and crack for a moment before the spears shoot up, in a pattern a regular learns. Deadly.

**Rooms and loot.** Halls with spear fields guarding the finds. Rewards (its exotic secret):
Tidewalker Boots.
**Keep.** The Spearstone Shard unlocks its secret.
