# Tidepool Rocks (biome, common; zones 1 to 4)

**Identity.** Flat benches of dark rock stepping down the beach, pocked with pools full of
life. The pools keep their water after the sea goes, so a bench is a puzzle of where to step.

**Look.** Rock: Slate; floor: Sand between the benches. Clear blue haze. Decor: starfish,
urchins, shells, barnacles, weed in the pools; fishing lures snagged in the cracks.

**The frame.** Low benches (in zones 1 to 3 no higher than the zone allows, no tall walls),
each holding several pools; sand gullies between them.
- **The entrance**: a blowhole in the rocks (it spouts as the tide comes in).

**The paths.** Zones 1 to 3 open; zone 4 two paths: down the gullies (carts) or across the
benches pool to pool (foot only, quicker).

**Dangers.** Gentle: none deadly. The pools are deep enough to wade slowly; a bench's pools
cut you off as the tide turns.

**Rooms and loot.** Sheltered pools in the benches. Loot: Seashell, Starfish, Fishing Lure.
**Keep.** The secret: the Pearl-Eyed Shell unlocks it; its coin is blue.
