# Whirlwind Dunes (biome, rare; zones 4 to 6)

**Identity.** Tall dunes the wind has carved into a maze, and a whirlwind that circles them all
tide, lifting sand and anything loose.

**Look.** Floor and walls: Sand (the dunes; packed sand walls in zones 4 to 6). Hot ochre haze,
clear. Decor: wind-worn posts, sand ripples, bleached bones, flags.

**The frame.** Dune ridges in a maze; a bowl in the middle where the whirlwind turns.

**The paths.** Two or three per crossing: one through the bowl (quick, across the whirlwind's
circle), others round the dunes.

**Dangers.** **The whirlwind** (a new hazard kind): it circles a fixed route on a rhythm you can
read (the sand it lifts shows where it is from far off); caught, you're flung and drop what
you hold, and a loaded cart is spilled. Not deadly itself; flung into a pit or the water, it is.

**Rooms and loot.** Hollows in the dunes. Rewards (its exotic secret): Magic Carpet, Gust Gloves.
**Keep.** The Wind Chime unlocks its secret.
