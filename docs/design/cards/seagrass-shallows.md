# Seagrass Shallows (biome, common; zones 1 to 4)

**Identity.** Low, green, half-flooded flats where seagrass grows thick, cut by shallow
channels; an old jetty collapsed across it. The first place the sea comes back to.

**Look.** Floor: Leafy Grass (seagrass) and mud. Green haze. Decor: seagrass, buoys, nets,
glass floats, jetty posts and planks.

**The frame.** Flats with channels across (they flood in stages as the tide comes in); the
collapsed jetty as the landmark.
- **The entrance**: under the collapsed jetty.
- **Old Nell, net-mender** (trader) sets up here.

**The paths.** Zones 1 to 3 open; zone 4 two paths: round the channels (carts) or through
them (foot, quick while they're dry).

**Dangers.** Gentle: **tangling seagrass** patches slow you (the existing Slow hazard); the
channels flood first.

**Rooms and loot.** Pockets in the grass, under the jetty. Loot: Buoy, Net, Glass Float.
**Keep.** The secret: the Barnacled Lantern unlocks it; its coin is green.

**Questions.**
1. Seagrass slowing you: enough, or should the channels' undertow join it in zone 4?
