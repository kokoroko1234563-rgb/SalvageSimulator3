# The shore (zones 1 to 3; built)

**Identity.** A sunny, inviting shore that gets wilder going out: dunes and a creek (zone 1),
curving sandbars and runnels (zone 2), bigger banks, a creek mouth and a half-buried wreck
(zone 3). Built, natural, never repeated.

**Paths.** Open zones: one wide path each, carts everywhere. Times measure about 30 s in each:
- zone 1: 30 of 35 (86%), within the rules;
- zone 2: 30 of 40 (75%), on the edge;
- zone 3: 30 of 45 (67%), under the minimum.

Low banks can't slow a walker (terrain rounds them), and rock isn't allowed before zone 4.

**Questions.**
1. Zone 3: allow a few rock outcrops (walls) to lengthen the way, or lower its target to 40?

Answer: Lower the target, no walls that high should be present here

3. Dangers on the shore: none (as now), or the first gentle one in zone 3?

Answer: Let's not have dangers here, if it seems necessary they can be added way later
