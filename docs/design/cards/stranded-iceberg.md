# Stranded Iceberg (biome, very rare; zones 5 and 6 only)

**Identity.** An iceberg has run aground on the beach: a mountain of blue ice with crevasses,
ice tunnels and a frozen lake, cold mist pouring off it. Nothing like anything else down here.

**Look.** Floor: Snow; rock: Glacier ice. Pale blue haze, brighter than its zone. Decor:
icicles, frost patches, frozen fish, a lost penguin's things.

**The frame.** The berg across the zone: ice walls and tunnels, crevasses, the frozen lake.
- **Pip, a lost penguin** (trader) sets up here: Frostrunner Wax for Frozen Fish.

**The paths.** Two or three per crossing: across the frozen lake (quick, slippery), through the
ice tunnels (dark, crevasses), round the berg (long, the carts').

**Dangers.**
- **Slippery ice** (a new hazard kind): on ice you slide, slow to stop and turn; a cart slides
  further and spills what it carries if it hits a wall.
- **Crevasses**: covered pits under thin ice (the cracks are the tell), open ones you slide into.
- The cold mist hides the far side.

**Rooms and loot.** Ice caves. Loot: Frozen Fish. Rewards (its exotic secret): Frost Sled,
Freeze Wand; the Frosted variant.
**Keep.** The Frozen Compass unlocks its secret.
