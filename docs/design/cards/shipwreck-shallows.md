# Shipwreck Shallows (biome, uncommon; zones 2 to 6)

**Identity.** A wreck-strewn stretch around one big wreck broken on the sand: scour channels,
debris mounds, cargo everywhere. The barrels still rolling off her deck down the gullies.

**Look.** Floor: Pavement (tarry, timber-strewn sand). Rock: Slate. A brown, smoky haze.
Decor: timber, ribs, crates, chains, an anchor, rope, the wreck itself as the landmark.

**The frame.**
- **The wreck**: one big hull lying across the zone; in zones 4 to 6 its hull is a wall you go
  round or through (its hatch is the biome's entrance), in zones 2 and 3 it lies low (no walls
  before zone 4).
- Scour channels round the wreck, debris mounds, gullies running down the zone.

**The paths.** Five crossings per zone. Path counts: 2, 2, 1, 3, 2.
- Paths differ by **the barrels**: one down a gully (quick, the barrels come down it), one
  along the mounds (slow, safe), and in the three-path crossing one through the hull.
- Links at the scour channels' crossings.

**Dangers.**
- **Barrels (Roller)** rolling down the gullies on a timer: readable (you hear and see them
  come). Today they knock what you hold out of your hands. Under the "deadly" rule they should
  do more (see questions).
- **Undertow** in the scour channels; **covered pits** in the debris (rotten decking).
- A regular learns: the barrels' rhythm, which gullies they use.

**Rooms and loot.** The wreck's holds (dead ends inside the hull, zones 4 to 6), cargo piles,
a flooded scour hole. The ShipBell and the best crates in the holds.

**Keep.** The hatch (entrance) spots. The rock pools.

**Questions.**
