# Driftwood Cove (biome, common; zones 1 to 4)

**Identity.** A log jam: the sea has piled whole trees across the beach, bleached and tangled.
You thread the gaps, climb the low logs, and the water pools behind them.

**Look.** Floor: Sand, darker and wetter behind the jams. Rock (zone 4): weathered wood-grey.
Warm haze. Decor: driftwood, timber, rope, old boots, glass bottles; a beached tree with roots.

**The frame.** Jams of logs across the zone (low ridges in zones 1 to 3, no tall walls; true
walls only in zone 4), each with gaps; dips behind them that hold water.
- **The entrance**: a hollow beached tree trunk (the secret's door).

**The paths.** Zones 1 to 3 open (one wide path, carts everywhere); zone 4 two paths: through
the gaps (carts) or over the low logs (foot only, quicker).

**Dangers.** Gentle (a common biome near the shop): none deadly.
- The pools behind the jams flood early and hold you back; a log stack that shifts and knocks
  you down when you climb it (zone 4)?

**Rooms and loot.** Hollows under the jams. Loot: Plank, Rope, Glass Bottle, Old Boot.
**Keep.** The secret: the Carved Idol unlocks it; its coin is orange.
