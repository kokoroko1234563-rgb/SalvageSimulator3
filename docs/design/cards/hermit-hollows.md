# Hermit Hollows (biome, rare; zones 4 to 6)

**Identity.** A giant hermit crab's warren: a maze of roofed tunnels in shell-crusted rock,
crab nests, middens of empty shells. You are in someone's home, and it knows.

**Look.** Floor: Concrete (crushed shell grit). Rock: Cobblestone (shell-crusted). Decor: shell
middens, crab moults, bones, glowing shells marking the safe ways in the dark.

**The frame (per zone).**
- Tunnels 12 to 16 wide, mostly roofed, opening into a few chambers where the roof breaks.
- **The Lair**: a big chamber where the giant crab's route begins (zones 5 and 6).
- One entrance flat in a chamber, for the biome's secret.

**The paths.** Five crossings per zone. Path counts: 2, 2, 3, 1, 2 (zone 4); deeper zones lean
to 2 and 3 (the warren is a web).
- Paths differ by **who patrols them**: one along the crabs' patrols (timed), one past the
  giant's tunnel (sneak or outrun), one long way through chambers.
- Links in the chambers.
- The cart path: the widest tunnels (16), avoiding the giant's.

**Dangers.**
- **Patrol** crabs (16 to 24 in a zone) on routes that cross the paths: time them.
- **The giant crab (Hunter)**: zones 5 and 6, one per crossing, down a long straight tunnel it
  fills: it sees ahead in a cone, so you cross behind it or outrun it. It sees lanterns far off.
- **Covered pits** in the middens.
- A regular learns: the patrols' timing, the giant's tunnel and when its back is turned,
  that a lantern is a beacon to it.

**Rooms and loot.** Crab nests (dead ends with big shells), shell middens, a collapsed chamber.
The BigShell finds in the nests.

**Keep.** The entrance flat. Zone 4's warren as built (it plays), extended with paths.
