# Jellyfish Lagoon (biome, uncommon; zones 2 to 6)

**Identity.** One big lagoon held in a ring of sand, causeways across it, the water purple and
glowing with jellyfish. Beautiful, and every step near the water is a risk.

**Look.** Floor: Sand and mud. Purple haze, darker than its zone. Decor: glowing jellies
stranded on the sand, weed, shells.

**The frame.** The lagoon (a hollow that holds water, flooding from its seaward side first),
its ring, causeways across it (some foot-only).

**The paths.** One round the ring (long, dry, the carts'), one along the causeways (quick,
through the jellies), and in deeper zones a third through the lagoon itself at low tide.

**Dangers.** **Jellyfish** drifting in the water (a new hazard kind): a sting makes you drop
what you hold (zones 2 to 4); in zones 5 and 6 a sting also stuns you for a moment, so with the
tide in, it can drown you. Their glow is the tell.

**Rooms and loot.** Coves round the lagoon. Rewards (its exotic secret): Jelly Raft, Glow Pearl.
**Keep.** The Jelly Jar unlocks its secret.
