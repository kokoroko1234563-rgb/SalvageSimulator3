# Coral Garden (biome, common; zones 1 to 4)

**Identity.** A dried-out reef: spines of pink and white coral standing in pairs, arches
between them, coconuts and snorkels left by someone who came before.

**Look.** Floor: Sand; the coral as pale rock (pink in the light). Pink haze, bright and
saturated. Decor: coral, dead coral, starfish, coconuts, a snorkel.

**The frame.** Reef spines in pairs with arches between them to walk through (low in zones
1 to 3; walls in zone 4).
- **The entrance**: an arch in the reef.

**The paths.** Zones 1 to 3 open; zone 4 two paths: round the reef (carts) or through the
arches (foot, quicker).

**Dangers.** Gentle: **sharp coral** beds that knock you back if you run through them (an
existing Knockdown patch), easy to step round.

**Rooms and loot.** Coves between the spines. Loot: Coral, Snorkel, Coconut.
**Keep.** The secret: the Coral Comb unlocks it; its coin is pink.
