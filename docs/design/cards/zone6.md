# Zone 6: The Drowned Reach (target 85 s)

**Identity.** The deep end: a sunken maze of galleries and halls, more roof than sky, black
water, the fog at its thickest. Something lives in the dark (the Gloom). The last zone before
the Far Sandbar, and the hardest: a test of everything the zones above taught.

**Look.** Floor: Limestone. Rock: Basalt and Slate by ward. Fog thickest. Decor: wreck ribs,
bones, chains and an anchor, dead coral, barnacled walls; lantern posts and glowing weed and
shells as the only lights.

**The frame.**
- Not a grid. Galleries of different widths and lengths, halls (big roofed chambers with
  pillars), and a few open-sky courts where light falls through.
- Landmarks: **the Drowned Hall** (a pillared chamber in the middle), **the Stair** (a long
  gallery that steps down), **the Wreck Court** (an open court with a wreck in it).
- At most about 40% roofed.

**The paths.** Five crossings. Path counts: 2, 3, 2, 1, 3.
- Paths differ in **light**: a lit path (lantern posts all the way, long), a dark path through
  the Gloom's galleries (short, if you have a lantern and nerve), and in three-path crossings a
  path through the Drowned Hall (rockfall).
- The one-path crossing: a single long winding way with every danger on it.
- Paths cross in the courts (open sky: light, a place to regroup).
- The cart's path is the lit one.

**Dangers** (the zone's own kit: Sink, Rockfall, Undertow, Gloom, Pit).
- **The Gloom**: its own danger. One or two per crossing, walking the dark galleries; its
  shadow Benights you. Wards (lantern posts, glowing weed) light the safe ways.
- **Rockfall** in the halls; **sinking sand** in the lanes to dead ends; **undertow** in the
  channels; **covered spike pits** under the best finds.
- A regular learns: where the Gloom walks and when to cross its gallery, the lit way when
  they're without a lantern, which prizes have cracks round them.

**Rooms and loot.** Seven slots: a pillared chamber, a flooded vault, a sinking flat, a tunnel
ring, a comb of caves. Best finds deepest, the very best on covered pits.

**Keep.** The Far Sandbar's approach (the zone's bottom edge) open: it leads straight to the
channel.

**Decided.**
- Rebuilt with the kit (`tools/kit/plans/zone6.py`), and it's **the role model**: the plan every
  other zone and biome copies (how crossings are laid out, rooms meet doors, landmarks and tells
  are placed).
