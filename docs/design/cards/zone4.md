# Zone 4: Outer Shore (target 55 s)

**Identity.** Where rock starts. The sand gives way to tall weed-stained rock ribs running
across the beach, with sandy lanes between them: the first place you can't see where you're
going. Still mostly open sky; the first taste of a maze, readable enough to learn quickly.

**Look.** Floor: Limestone (drowned grey silt). Rock: Basalt. Fog medium. Decor: kelp and weed
heaped at the rib bases, barnacles on the rock, driftwood and timber jammed in the gaps, the odd
crate. Each rib a different silhouette (one with an arch, one broken into stacks, one bent).

**The frame.**
- Three rock ribs across the zone, each broken in several places (doors a crossing may open or
  plug), each with its own landmark: **the Arch** (a short roofed passage through the middle
  rib), **the Stacks** (a rib broken into sea stacks at one end), **the Bend** (a rib that curls
  back on itself into a cove).
- Side pockets along both edges, walled off from the lanes.

**The paths.** Five crossings. Path counts by crossing: 2, 1, 2, 3, 2.
- The one-path crossing goes the long way round through the Arch.
- In two-path crossings: one wide path (the cart's) that winds through the ribs' main doors,
  and one foot path that scrambles through narrow breaks in a rib (6 to 8 wide: foot and Small
  carts only) but has to cross the rockfall stretch.
- The three-path crossing: the cart's, a foot path through the Stacks, and a path along the
  west edge through two side pockets linked by a squeeze.
- Paths link up in the lanes between ribs (each lane is shared open ground for a stretch).

**Dangers** (the zone's own kit, new: `Config.Zones[4].Hazards`).
- **Rockfall**: its own danger. One stretch per rib where the rock overhangs (the rib's breaks
  under the Arch and the Bend's inside), strewn with rubble. The quick path always crosses one.
- **Spike pits**, open, in the narrow breaks: step round them, and they cost a little time.
- **Undertow** along the flooding channels at the lowest lane.
- A regular learns: which breaks have rockfall and how to time them, which way round the pits.

**Rooms and loot.** Six side pockets: a dead-end cave under a rib, a tide pool, a boulder
scramble, a drift of timber and crates (a wreck yard). The best finds deep in the caves; one
covered pit under a prize per crossing (the tell: cracks).

**Keep.** The rock pool at (260, z 70 in the zone) clear by 6. The cart route 14+ wide.
