# Sandcastle Flats (biome, common; zones 1 to 4)

**Identity.** A holiday beach gone grand: sandcastles everywhere, from buckets to towering
forts with moats, flags and walls of sand. Bright, warm, open. The first biome most players meet.

**Look.** Floor: Sand. Bright warm haze. Decor: buckets and spades, flags, shells on the castle
walls, towels.

**The frame.**
- Castles of every size; in zones 3 and 4 a **great castle** whose walls and moat make a small
  maze (sand walls: ridges, no rock before zone 4).
- The secret's sandcastle on one of the entrance flats.

**The paths.** Zones 1 and 2: open (one path, the flats are open sand). Zones 3 and 4: five
crossings, path counts 2, 1, 2, 2, 3: round the great castle's moat, through its gate, over its
walls (foot only).

**Dangers.** Gentle by design (the first biome, zones 1 to 4).
- **Moats**: water-filled cuts round the castles that flood early (undertow when the sea is in).
- **Collapsing walls**: covered pits in the great castle's courtyard (a castle's sand gives way).
- Questions below.

**Rooms and loot.** The castles' courtyards and towers. The GoldenSpade's secret as built.

**Keep.** The entrance flats and the secret's placement.

**Questions.**
1. Should Sandcastle Flats have deadly dangers at all, being the holiday biome in zones 1 to 4?

Answer: No, I do not think it should have any very deadly dangers, but should instead have some pretty hidden secrets, along with entering the castle at all being optional rather than required. I do think there should be traps that throw the player out of the castle though upon failure, like hidden trap-doors and walls, I also think this will require making the engine possible to create multiple layers, so maybe we should discuss it and weather it is a good idea at all.
